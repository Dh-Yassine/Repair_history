Main idea 
AutoHistory is a cloud-based and mobile platform that lets owners create a fully auditable record of 
every maintenance event their vehicles experience—oil changes, tire rotations, repairs, and more—
by uploading receipts, photos and notes linked to mileage. The system automatically pulls vehicle 
data via VIN (or allows manual entry) and provides authorized repair shops with instant access to the 
history so they can add a “verified & performed by professional” tag on new service entries. This 
creates an immutable, shop-validated maintenance trail that buyers can view through an embeddable 
badge on listings, giving them confidence in the vehicle’s condition while helping sellers demonstrate 
continuous care and potentially command higher sale prices.
